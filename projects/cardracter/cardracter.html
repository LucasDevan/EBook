02 / DEEP DIVE

The technical difficulties we met along the way

Data-base

What implementing a complex data-base in a mobile app taught us?

At the start of the project me and my team mate wanted a data base that would match our ambitions by making it very flexible and futur proof for any further devellopement

However having data classes inheriting from a common abstract data class act proved difficult as the right tools to create those abstract data classes and differentiate between the sub-classes didn't exist in Kotlin

The subsequent very complex implentation made any further developpement much more complicated as it required changing lots of complex and compact code while adding a lot of constraints on how to operate it

This mistake taught me and my class mate important lessons about addapting our ambitions not only to our current abilities but also to the platform we were developing on.

This is why when making the FLutter version of our app we implemented a simplified version of our database that lost some felxibility but became much eassier to implement and to interact with.

User accounts

How we implemented user registration, loggin and data usage

As CardRacter was made with the goal of enabling it's users to create, save and share cards, it was very important for the app to have a distant data-base and accounts to let our usesrs save their creation in a secure manner

To this end we implemened an account creation system
